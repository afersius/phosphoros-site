Privacy policy

Phosphoros Uploader and Phosphoros Publisher · Last updated 10 November 2026

What these tools are

Phosphoros Uploader and Phosphoros Publisher are internal tools used only by the Phosphoros channel. They upload and schedule Phosphoros's own videos to its own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, and to its own Instagram account, through the Instagram API; they help the channel answer comments on its own videos and posts; and they read the channel's own statistics. They are not offered to other users.

What data they access

The channel's own content: video files, titles, descriptions, captions and cover images that Phosphoros created, and the status of those uploads. The channel's own statistics (views, watch time, likes, followers), in aggregate. Public comments left on Phosphoros's own videos and posts: the commenter's public display name and the text of the comment, which are shown privately to the channel's owner and used to draft a reply. Drafts are written with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), which receives only the comment text, the display name and the video title. No reply is published without the owner's manual approval. Comments are kept on the channel's own computer only to avoid showing the same comment twice. No personal data is sold or shared for any other purpose, and these tools use no cookies or tracking.

How access is stored

The authorization tokens for the channel's own accounts are kept on the channel's own computer and as encrypted secrets in its private automation settings. They are never published or shared. Notices for the owner (new comments, published videos) are sent to the owner's private Telegram chat.

YouTube API Services

Phosphoros Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
